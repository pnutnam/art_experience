## ⚠ One arithmetic flag found in review — FIXED

**#33 "Twenty-three years after the speech":** Gettysburg Address Nov 19, 1863;
*Bosses of the Senate* Jan 23, 1889 → **25 years, 2 months**. The narration said
"twenty-three." This is wrong and is corrected to "twenty-five" in `tour.json`; the
corrected MP3 was pending regeneration while the TTS endpoint was unreachable (the
app falls back to the device voice for that one beat meanwhile — re-run
`build/generate_audio.py` when convenient to restore the recorded voice). All other
date arithmetic above is either exact or flagged 🟡 with the rounding noted ("four
years," "another twenty years" — both spoken-rounding, kept).
